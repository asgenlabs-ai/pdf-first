Plain-language summary: PDF First is designed to process core document tasks on your device where applicable. Your selected PDFs and scanned document contents are not intentionally uploaded to ASGENLABS servers simply to perform those local tools. The app may still use internet-connected third-party services such as Google AdMob for advertising, and those services may process data under their own policies.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how PDF First, developed and published by ASGENLABS, handles information when you use the application. We aim to minimize unnecessary access to user files while providing PDF creation, scanning, conversion, editing and document-management features.

2. Document Processing

Core operations such as scanning, viewing, creating, merging, splitting, compressing, converting, signing and editing PDF documents are designed to be performed locally on the Android device where the feature implementation permits it.

PDF First does not intentionally upload the contents of PDFs, scanned pages, signatures or locally selected images to ASGENLABS-operated servers merely to perform these local document operations.

3. Information and Data

PDF First itself does not require you to create an ASGENLABS account to use its core document tools. However, third-party SDKs used by the app, particularly advertising services, may automatically process technical information such as IP address, device or advertising identifiers, app interactions, diagnostics and related advertising data.

Exact data processing by third-party SDKs is governed by their own documentation, your region, consent choices and device settings.

4. Permissions

PDF First may request permissions only when needed for a feature you choose to use. Examples include camera access for scanning and access to user-selected files or media for opening, converting, inserting, exporting or saving documents.

You can manage or revoke Android permissions at any time through your device settings. Some features may stop working if required permissions are denied.

5. Files, Photos and Storage

When you select a PDF, image or other supported document, the app accesses that content to perform the action you requested. Files created or edited by PDF First may be stored in app storage or a location selected through Android's storage/file interfaces.

When you explicitly share or export a file through another application, that third-party application or service receives the content you chose to share.

6. Signatures and Annotations

Signature drawings, annotations, highlights, text, images and other editing objects may be created and embedded into documents. These items are intended to remain local to the device/document unless you export or share the resulting file.

7. Advertising — Google AdMob

PDF First uses Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process information for ad delivery, consent management, measurement, fraud prevention and related purposes.

This can include device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address or general location inferred from it, app interactions, diagnostics and ad-performance information, depending on your region, consent status and Google settings.

Where required, privacy and consent choices may be presented before applicable advertising requests. ASGENLABS does not control Google's independent data-processing practices.

8. Internet and Network Access

Core document operations may work locally, while an internet connection can still be used for advertising, consent-management components, external resources, SDK communication or other network-enabled functionality included in the app.

The existence of network access does not mean that your local PDF document contents are automatically uploaded to ASGENLABS.

9. Third-Party Services

The application may include Android/Google platform components, Google Mobile Ads and other software libraries required for app functionality. Third-party services may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

10. Data Retention

Documents created or edited with PDF First are generally retained on your device or in a storage location you choose until you delete or move them. ASGENLABS does not intentionally maintain server-side copies of locally processed document contents unless a future feature clearly informs you otherwise.

Android backup or device-transfer behavior can depend on the app version, Android configuration and backup rules.

11. Security

We use reasonable technical practices to limit unnecessary access to user files and permissions. However, no application, device, electronic storage method or transmission system can guarantee absolute security.

You are responsible for protecting access to your device and for choosing secure destinations when exporting or sharing sensitive documents.

12. Children's Privacy

PDF First is a general-purpose productivity/document utility and is not specifically designed to solicit personal information from children. If the app's audience or data practices change, this policy will be updated accordingly.

13. Your Choices

You can manage camera, file/media and other Android permissions from your device settings. Advertising and privacy controls may also be available through Android, Google settings or consent interfaces shown by the app where required.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when PDF First features, third-party SDKs, legal requirements or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with a revised update date.
