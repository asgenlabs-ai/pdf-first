Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how PDF First handles documents, permissions, local processing, advertising, and information used by third-party services.

1. Introduction

PDF First is a document utility application developed and published by ASGENLABS. This policy describes how information is handled when you use the app.

We aim to request only the access needed for features you choose to use and to keep document processing local where the feature is implemented on-device.

2. Information We Process

PDF First does not intentionally require you to create an ASGENLABS account for its core document tools. Depending on the feature you use, the app may process:

  • PDFs, images, scans and other documents you choose.
  • Signatures, annotations, notes, text and edits you create.
  • File names and local file metadata needed to display, save or organize files.
  • Camera input when you use document scanning.
  • Advertising, device, diagnostic and interaction information processed by Google AdMob and related Google services.

3. Documents & Local Processing

Core PDF features are designed to run on your Android device where applicable, including scanning, viewing, creating, converting, merging, splitting, compressing, signing and editing documents.

PDF First does not intentionally upload your document contents to ASGENLABS servers solely to perform these local tools. Files remain under your control unless you choose to save, export or share them using Android or another app/service.

Important Some app components may use network access for advertising, consent, updates or external resources. Therefore, “local document processing” should not be interpreted as “the app never connects to the internet.”

4. Permissions

Camera

Camera access is requested only when you use document scanning. Images captured for scanning are processed for the requested document workflow.

Files, photos and documents

PDF First may access files or images you select through Android's system file/photo picker or other permitted storage mechanisms so it can open, edit, convert, save or share them.

You can review or revoke app permissions at any time from Android Settings, although some features may stop working without the required permission.

5. Files & Storage

Generated or edited documents may be stored on your device, in app-specific storage, or in a location you choose. Android system backup or device-transfer features may apply to app data depending on the app's release configuration and your Android/Google backup settings.

If you share a file using Android's share sheet, the selected third-party app receives that file and its own privacy policy applies.

6. Signatures & Annotations

Signatures, drawings, highlights, text, sticky notes and other annotations you create are used to produce or edit documents. These items are intended to be handled locally as part of the document workflow unless you export or share the resulting file.

7. Advertising (Google AdMob)

PDF First uses Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads to display advertising. Google may process information such as IP address, device or advertising identifiers, app interactions, diagnostic information and ad performance data for purposes including ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and compliance.

Depending on your region and Google settings, consent or privacy choices may be presented before applicable advertising requests. Google's own privacy terms and policies govern Google's processing.

8. Internet & External Resources

An internet connection may be used for advertising, consent management, externally hosted resources, updates or other network-dependent components included in the app. Network access does not by itself mean that your PDF/document contents are uploaded to ASGENLABS.

9. Third-Party Services

PDF First may include Android/Google platform services, Google Mobile Ads and software libraries needed for PDF, image, camera and document functionality. Third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

10. Data Retention

Documents and files you create or edit generally remain on your device or at the storage location you choose until you delete them. ASGENLABS does not intentionally maintain server-side copies of locally processed document contents for the app's core offline tools.

Third-party services may retain data they process according to their own policies.

11. Security

We use reasonable technical and product-design measures intended to limit unnecessary access to your documents and app data. However, no application, device, network or storage system can guarantee absolute security.

You are responsible for protecting access to your device and for choosing where exported documents are stored or shared.

12. Children's Privacy

PDF First is a general-purpose productivity/document utility and is not specifically directed to children. ASGENLABS does not intentionally operate a document-upload service designed to collect personal information from children through the app's core local PDF tools.

13. Your Choices

  • Manage camera and file/media permissions in Android Settings.
  • Choose which files or images you open in PDF First.
  • Choose whether and where to save or share generated files.
  • Use Google/Android privacy and advertising controls where available.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy when the app, SDKs, advertising configuration, legal requirements or privacy practices change. The current version will be published on this page with an updated date.
